02 — My Story

The Journey So Far

Age 12

First Taste of Real Work

Helped my father run his construction business back in Nepal, handling customer interactions, logistics, and learning what it means to take responsibility at a young age. That early exposure to real-world pressure shaped who I am today.

2024

The Decision to Leave

After 18 years of growing up in a comfortable, family-supported environment in Nepal, I made the decision to pursue Computer Science in the United States — alone. No safety net, just a plan and the will to make it work.

Jan 2025

Landed in the USA

Arrived in the United States on an F-1 student visa. Didn't know my roommate. Didn't fully know the system. Had to figure out banking, transportation, daily life, and college — all at once. The first months were a crash course in independence.

Spring 2025

First Semester — Forsyth Tech

Enrolled at Wright State University, Ohio. While adjusting to a completely new environment, I also started teaching myself web development on the side, using Claude, ChatGPT, and apps like SoloLearn — with zero formal coding classes.

Summer 2025

California — Visalia, Delano, Bakersfield, Fresno

Spent the summer break in California, moving through multiple cities — Visalia, Delano, Bakersfield, and Fresno. Gained a wider perspective on American culture, built new connections, and kept learning through every experience. Before being enrolled in Forsyth Tech Community College.

Fall 2025

First Real Projects — Shipped Live

Built two fully functional websites for a friend's franchise business — a phone repair shop site with a live booking system, and an employee payroll tracker. Both are live on the web. This was entirely self-taught, no classroom, no instructor.

2025–26

Independence - Credit, and Counting

Bought my own car in the US. Started actively building my credit score from scratch — learning the American financial system, understanding credit utilization, payment history, and long-term financial strategy. Handling while maintaining a full course load.

Now

Currently — Building, Learning, Growing

Second Semester at Forsyth Tech, deepening my Python skills, working on new projects, and looking for internship opportunities. Every challenge I've faced has added a layer of maturity and grit that no classroom can teach.
