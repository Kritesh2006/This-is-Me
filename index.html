      About

      I moved from Nepal to the United States alone at eighteen, in January 2025, and have been learning two things at once ever since: how a new country works, and how to build software that holds up.

      Most of what I know I taught myself before a class asked me to. I learn by shipping something small, watching where it breaks, and fixing it. That is how a timesheet for coworkers turned into a parts-lookup tool the whole shop now uses.

      Before code, I fixed phones — first at a repair counter in Kathmandu, now in Greensboro. I still do. It taught me to diagnose before guessing, which turns out to be most of software too. I also play music and speak four languages.
