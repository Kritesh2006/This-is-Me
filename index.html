Profile · Background

Background & Philosophy

I'm Kritesh Dhungel, a full-stack engineer based in Greensboro, North Carolina. My work sits where operations meet software: I start from a real bottleneck, ship the smallest thing that removes it, watch where it breaks, and harden it.

That loop turned a spreadsheet people scrolled through into a ranked search engine staff rely on every day. On the AI side I build orchestration, not wrappers — models working in parallel, a judge that weighs their output, and deterministic layers wherever correctness matters more than fluency.

“I build systems that hold up under daily use, then keep improving them based on how people actually use them.”
  1. 01

    Start from a real bottleneck

    Watch the work before writing code. The problem decides the stack.

  2. 02

    Ship the smallest fix

    Get it into real hands fast, then let usage tell me what matters.

  3. 03

    Watch where it breaks

    Edge cases from real users beat imagined requirements.

  4. 04

    Harden it

    Deterministic paths, tests and clean boundaries once it earns its place.
