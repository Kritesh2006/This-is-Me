FEATURED · AUTONOMOUS AI SYSTEM

ARIA

Autonomous Reasoning & Intelligent AI Assistant

ARIA is not a chatbot. It is a multi-agent reasoning system where four specialized AI agents — Strategist, Critic, Realist, and Judge — operate in parallel, actively debate each other's outputs, and converge on the most defensible answer. Built for high-stakes analytical use: investment research, financial decision support, and expense intelligence. The long-term vision is a personal operating system that connects to your financial accounts, understands your spending patterns, and proactively tells you where to cut — before you have to ask. Currently live with real-time web search, weather API integration, and voice interaction via Web Speech API.
